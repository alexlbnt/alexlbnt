## ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/◈-8B5CF6?style=flat-square&labelColor=0D1117) About Me

I'm a **Computer Science student and technology professional** interested in building useful software, understanding complex systems, and turning real-world challenges into effective solutions.

I work at **Centi Gestão Pública**, supporting and implementing management systems for public administration. My experience involves technical analysis, troubleshooting, requirements gathering, user training, and helping public-sector teams use technology more effectively.

Working at the intersection of **technology, business processes, and people** has strengthened my analytical thinking, communication, problem-solving, and organizational skills.

Beyond my professional responsibilities, I enjoy exploring software architecture, backend development, mobile applications, and user experience. I'm also building skills in **project management, planning, and team coordination**, with the long-term goal of leading technology projects and delivering solutions that create meaningful impact.

### What I'm focused on

- ▸ **Software Development:** Building maintainable applications and exploring scalable architectures.
- ▸ **Systems Analysis:** Understanding requirements, investigating technical issues, and improving business processes.
- ▸ **Project Management:** Developing skills in planning, organization, communication, and project execution.
- ▸ **Product & UX:** Designing digital experiences that balance usability, functionality, and technical quality.
- ▸ **Continuous Learning:** Applying academic knowledge to practical projects and real-world challenges.

### Featured Project

**Upon — Full-stack & Mobile Application**

I'm developing Upon as a practical project that brings together backend services, mobile development, and user experience.

The project is an opportunity to apply software engineering principles, explore architectural decisions, and improve my ability to develop and organize a complete software solution.

**Tech:** NestJS • React Native • TypeScript
