## ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/◈-8B5CF6?style=flat-square&labelColor=0D1117) About Me

I'm a **Systems Analyst at Centi Gestão Pública**, where I work with management software for municipal governments, mainly on the **Payroll** module, and a **Computer Science student at PUC Goiás** (graduating in 2027).

Day to day, I translate payroll legislation and business rules into **C# formulas** that calculate payment events, investigate calculation issues, and support public-sector teams using the system.

I've also served as **Implementation Lead** on two system rollouts for city governments, coordinating small teams, meeting with municipal leadership and accountants, following up with each department, and managing **data migration** from legacy systems.

My goal is to grow into **Project Management**, combining a solid technical foundation with planning, requirements gathering, and team coordination.

### 🔭 Right now

- ⚙️ Writing and maintaining **C# payroll formulas** (taxes, vacations, 13th salary, terminations)
- 🚀 Leading **system implementations** and data migration for public administration clients
- 📱 Building **Upon**, a full-stack & mobile app with NestJS and React Native
- 📚 Studying Computer Science and **project management** practices (Scrum, requirements engineering)
