## ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/◈-8B5CF6?style=flat-square&labelColor=0D1117) About Me

I am a Systems Analyst currently working with public sector management software, and I am also a **Computer Science** student.

I have served as a **project lead** for two system implementation projects for municipal governments, where I coordinated teams, held meetings with municipal officials and accountants, oversaw various departments, and managed data migration from legacy systems.

My goal is to advance into the field of **Project Management**, combining a solid technical foundation with expertise in planning, requirements gathering, and team coordination.
